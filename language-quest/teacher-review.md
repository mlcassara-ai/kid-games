## Questions for you

### Names and words that need your choice

1. **The grandparent characters** are now الجَدَّة (the grandmother) and المُخْتَار (the Mukhtar, who gives daily quests). Are these right for your students?
2. **Hamzat al-wasl.** Some words are written with a kasra on the first alif (اِثْنَان، الاِتِّحَاد). Standard spelling leaves it bare. Which do you prefer for teaching? It will be made the same everywhere.
3. **Alif examples.** The Alphabet School uses the sound أَ and the word أَسَد for alif, which really show hamza on alif. Would you prefer a word where alif is plainly a long "aa" (for example عَصَا at the end)? Also, ك is listed as a look-alike for ا; should it be removed?
4. **Tanween.** All three kinds are labelled تَنْوِين. Name them separately (تَنْوِينُ الفَتْح، الكَسْر، الضَّمّ)?
5. **Letter Friends with borrowed names:** شَمْبَانْزِي (ش), غُورِيلَّا (غ), لَامَا (ل). Swap for native animals such as شَحْرُور، غُرَاب، لَقْلَق?
6. **Colours** in the Tailor: two swatches do not match their names (the "green" is teal, the "red" is raspberry). We will change the colours to match the words unless you prefer other words.
7. **حَقِيبَة** is labelled "Backpack" in English; it means "bag". Change the word to حَقِيبَةُ ظَهْر or the label to "Bag"?
8. **Numbers quest word:** أَرْقَام (digits). Would أَعْدَاد be better for number words?
9. **Two fountain sayings are not traditional Arabic proverbs:** العَقْلُ السَّلِيمُ فِي الجِسْمِ السَّلِيم (from Latin) and الوَقْتُ مِنْ ذَهَب (modern). Keep or replace?
10. **Picture mismatches:** a graduate picture for تِلْمِيذ, a lotion bottle for صَمْغ, and the abstract word حَظّ as an example word. Better words?

### Please check these against how you teach

- **Number words** 0–100 in the Souq, and counting dinars 1–10 at the fountain (دِينَارٌ وَاحِد، دِينَارَان، ثَلَاثَةُ دَنَانِير …).
- **The 26 fountain sayings:** see `fountain-sayings.md`.
- **The five stories:** see `story-harbor-stories.md`. Is story 5's ending (the lion jumps into the well) fine for young children?
- **Alphabet School:** each letter's picture word (the first example word from the Letter Dunes), and whether the sound should be the letter with fatha (بَ).
- **Letter tracing (Camp One):** stroke order is body first, then dots right to left, with the top dot of ث last; end shapes start from the joining stroke on the right.
- **Grandmother's basket** (opens after all 28 Letter Friends): one everyday object per letter: إِبْرِيق، بَاب، تُفَّاحَة، ثَوْب، جَرَس، حَقِيبَة، خُبْز، دَلْو، ذُرَة، رِيشَة، زَيْتُون، سَاعَة، شَمْعَة، صُنْدُوق، ضِمَادَة، طَبْل، ظَرْف، عِنَب، غَيْمَة، فِنْجَان، قَلَم، كُرَة، لَيْمُون، مِفْتَاح، نَجْمَة، هَدِيَّة، وَرْدَة، يَد. Better words for any letter, especially ث ذ ض ظ?
- **Case endings:** phrases keep their endings inside, but the last word and single words are read without one (pausal form). Is that the convention you want?
