# Language Quest Wisdom Fountain — sayings for review

For Aroub to review. These 26 sayings are in the game's Wisdom Fountain as of version 2026.10.01h; any change she asks for can be made afterwards.

Each toss gives one item, shown large in Arabic with harakat, spoken aloud, with the English underneath. Please mark each one: keep, change (and how), or drop. Corrections to harakat and wording are especially welcome. Everything here is meant to be everyday folk wisdom and wordplay, with nothing taken from scripture or hadith.

## Proverbs (14)

| # | Arabic | Literal meaning | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | العِلْمُ نُور | Knowledge is light. | Learning helps you see the world clearly. |
| 2 | الصَّبْرُ مِفْتَاحُ الفَرَج | Patience is the key to relief. | Good things come if you keep calm and wait. |
| 3 | الوَقْتُ مِنْ ذَهَب | Time is made of gold. | Don't waste your time; it's precious. |
| 4 | الصَّدِيقُ وَقْتَ الضِّيق | A friend is for the hard times. | A real friend helps when things are difficult. |
| 5 | مَنْ جَدَّ وَجَد | Whoever works hard, finds. | Effort gets results. |
| 6 | يَدٌ وَاحِدَةٌ لَا تُصَفِّق | One hand cannot clap. | We need each other to get things done. |
| 7 | الجَارُ قَبْلَ الدَّار | The neighbour before the house. | Good neighbours matter more than a fancy home. |
| 8 | خَيْرُ الكَلَامِ مَا قَلَّ وَدَلّ | The best speech is short and clear. | Say what you mean in few words. |
| 9 | العَقْلُ السَّلِيمُ فِي الجِسْمِ السَّلِيم | A healthy mind is in a healthy body. | Look after your body and your mind works better. |
| 10 | القِرْدُ فِي عَيْنِ أُمِّهِ غَزَال | In his mother's eyes, the monkey is a gazelle. | Mums think their own children are the most beautiful. |
| 11 | عُصْفُورٌ فِي اليَدِ خَيْرٌ مِنْ عَشَرَةٍ عَلَى الشَّجَرَة | A bird in the hand is better than ten in the tree. | What you already have beats what you only hope for. |
| 12 | خَيْرُ جَلِيسٍ فِي الزَّمَانِ كِتَاب | The best companion of all time is a book. | Books are wonderful company. |
| 13 | التَّكْرَارُ يُعَلِّمُ الشُّطَّار | Repetition teaches the clever ones. | Practising again and again is how you get good. |
| 14 | مَنْ زَرَعَ حَصَد | Whoever plants, harvests. | You get out what you put in. |

Spare proverbs if any above are dropped: الاِتِّحَادُ قُوَّة (Unity is strength), لِكُلِّ مُجْتَهِدٍ نَصِيب (Every hard worker gets a share), دِرْهَمُ وِقَايَةٍ خَيْرٌ مِنْ قِنْطَارِ عِلَاج (A little prevention beats a lot of cure).

## Riddles (6)

The child hears the riddle, then picks the answer from three pictures.

| # | Riddle | English | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | مَا هُوَ الشَّيْءُ الَّذِي لَهُ أَسْنَانٌ وَلَا يَعَضّ؟ | What has teeth but does not bite? | المُشْط — a comb |
| 16 | مَا هُوَ الشَّيْءُ الَّذِي لَهُ عَيْنٌ وَلَا يَرَى؟ | What has an eye but cannot see? | الإِبْرَة — a needle |
| 17 | مَا هُوَ البَيْتُ الَّذِي لَيْسَ لَهُ أَبْوَابٌ وَلَا نَوَافِذ؟ | What house has no doors and no windows? | البَيْضَة — an egg |
| 18 | مَا هُوَ الشَّيْءُ الَّذِي لَهُ أَوْرَاقٌ وَلَيْسَ شَجَرَة؟ | What has leaves (pages) but is not a tree? | الكِتَاب — a book |
| 19 | مَا هُوَ الشَّيْءُ الَّذِي يَتَكَلَّمُ كُلَّ لُغَاتِ العَالَم؟ | What speaks every language in the world? | الصَّدَى — an echo |
| 20 | أَخْضَرُ فِي الأَرْض، أَسْوَدُ فِي السُّوق، أَحْمَرُ فِي البَيْت. مَا هُوَ؟ | Green in the ground, black in the market, red at home. What is it? | الشَّاي — tea |

Note on 18: the riddle works in Arabic because وَرَق means both "leaves" and "paper".
